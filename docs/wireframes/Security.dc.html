01

Held as USDC, one for one

Every dollar in your balance is a USDC dollar, issued by Circle and held in a treasury contract on Arc testnet. Circle Gateway keeps it one balance across chains, and Gas Station pays network fees, so you never hold a gas token.

USDCArc testnetCircle Gateway
02

Rules live in the contract

Card and QR limits, holds and approval rules are enforced by the treasury contract, not only by the app. A payment that breaks a rule can't settle.

03

A second person signs

Payments over your limit, or to someone new, wait for an approver, who signs with a passkey. Nobody approves their own request, and requests expire after 7 days.

04

No surprise rates

The rate is locked for 60 seconds at review. When the lock runs out, the button becomes Refresh rate. We never charge on an expired quote.

05

One ledger, every hop

A double-entry ledger records every payment, fee and hold, onchain or simulated. Each receipt shows every hop with its status, and USDC payments show their transaction reference.

What Sancus isn't

Sancus isn't a bank and doesn't hold customer funds. Real banking, cards and cross-border payouts need licensed partners and regulatory approval, and we'd work with them before anyone's real money moved.
