Before you open an account

Is this a real bank account?

No. Sancus is a hackathon prototype. Balances are testnet USDC with no real value, and every rail other than USDC is simulated and labelled that way.

Where can I pay?

Brazil by Pix, Mexico by SPEI, the euro area by SEPA, Indonesia by QRIS, Thailand by PromptPay, the US by ACH, anywhere by wire, and any business with a USDC account.

How fast does money arrive?

Pix, QRIS, PromptPay and USDC arrive in seconds, SPEI in minutes, SEPA the same day, and ACH in 1–2 days. Review shows the arrival time before you pay.

What does it cost?

Each rail has its own fee, from $0.00 for USDC to $3.50 for a wire. You see the fee and the rate before you confirm. Rails and fees

Who holds the money?

Your balance is held as USDC, one for one, and the rules on it are enforced by a contract. How your money is kept
